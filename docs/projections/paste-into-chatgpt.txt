DraftKings desktop, only when asked for desktop. One line, then the phone lines under it.

https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/event/SLUG/EVENTID?outcomes=SELECTIONID

SLUG is the event name in lowercase. Spaces become hyphens. The characters space-at-space become -%40-. TB Buccaneers @ CHI Bears becomes tb-buccaneers-%40-chi-bears. EVENTID is events[].id. SELECTIONID is selections[].id. Several ids on the desktop line are joined with a comma. The phone still gets one line per id.
