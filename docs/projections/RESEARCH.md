# Research levels

Every game gets the four reads. Nothing else is automatic. A higher level is more work on the same reads. It is not a new model, and it does not change a Pass into a Bet.

| Level | Name | When | What the work is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | screen | The default. A Pass or a Lean that nobody named. A live game with no ticket. | The posted number against the four reads. Then stop. |
| 2 | log | The screen says Bet or Bet now, on a league with a public log. | The matching-slice games, before the link. Look for a game that is not this opponent or not this role. |
| 3 | personnel | The game was named, or a ticket is placed. | Sourced absences, debuts, a quoted workload, and a game script. |
| 4 | local | FCS, J1, J2, J3, or the same kind of league, once the game is a Bet or was named. | A club site, a league preview, or a regional paper. Record the source, the date, and only what it says. |

A Pass on a 50-game Saturday stays at screen. Naming Monday night climbs to personnel even when the screen is a Pass. An FCS Pass stays at screen. An FCS Bet climbs to local, not to a link.

The game script, the local note, and the log do not enter the four reads.
