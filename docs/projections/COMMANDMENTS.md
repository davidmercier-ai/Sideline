# Commandments

These govern every slate. The prediction comes first. The books' prices stay in their own view.

1. Open with the predicted slate: the venue-matched score, the total, the model vote, and the sim.
2. Keep three views apart: our projection, the books' numbers and prices with the time they were retrieved, and the comparison. The game log against the posted number stays with the comparison. It does not enter the projection.
3. Run four models and do not average them. Agreement is a vote among those models. It is not outside consensus.
4. Name a side only when every available model agrees. A split stays a split. Synergy stays visible as reinforce or diverge.
5. Run the sim on its own: 2,000 draws, a fixed seed, and no price read while the draws run. Mark it thin when any venue slice has fewer than 3 games.
6. Leave a missing number missing. Do not build a median, a mode, a high, a low, or a win probability out of an average.
7. Report FanDuel and DraftKings prices as their numbers, with the American price on both sides. FanDuel game lines, and the NFL props that board attaches, come from the Ohio page. DraftKings game lines and player over/unders come from the sportsbook-nash feed. The ESPN quote remains a DraftKings game line when that quote is present. Priced premade parlays come from the FanDuel betting-opportunities card and the DraftKings prepacks widget, both for Ohio. The combined price is that book's number for the whole parlay.
8. Rank a price only when both books post the same number. The better price is the higher American odds.
9. A book number outside every model is line value, a point gap. Leave a priced edge unlabeled.
10. Do not fill a prop, a team total, an opening price, or an outside pick that the source did not send. Do not fit a model to a book. One snapshot is not a closing line.
11. Record public consensus from handicappers, sports media, and public models in its own view. Name the source, the side, and the count. Do not blend that view into the four models or the book prices.
12. Write a store for every board. The markdown report is the reading copy. The store is `docs/projections/stores/<board-id>.json`, and `index.json` lists the boards. A board that is only in the chat is not stored.
13. After the book comparison, list each completed game the means came from: date, site, opponent, score, and margin. Count the matching slice against each book's spread and total. The matching slice is the visitor's away games and the home team's home games. On a neutral site the matching slice is every completed game, because the home/away split is the wrong site. The count is not a model and not an edge. A cover percentage is covers divided by decided games, and an over or under percentage is that result divided by decided games. A push is not decided. The percentage is stored with the team result record. It does not enter the four models.
14. Every alert names the bet, the price, a rating, and an action. A player prop also names the game: both teams and the kickoff. The bet comes first. The game follows the word Game, so a team name is not read as the venue. The rating is the log, from 5 down to 1. A 5 has every read on that side, a sample of at least 5, and room on the closest game. A 4 has the venue log on that side with room and one scar. A 3 has the venue log on that side, and the last three or one game misses. A 2 is a split, a sample under 3, or a live number one score from dead. A 1 has the log on the other side, or the live number is already behind. The action is Bet now, Bet, Lean, or Pass. A Bet or Bet now also carries the book link that opens that one selection. Several bets share one DraftKings slip. A message sends one `dksb://sb/addbet/` link per selection, and a hash is `%23`. The phone adds one pick per tap. A sportsbook.draftkings.com event link is not sent in a message. It opens the game and does not add the legs. `https://dksb.sng.link/` is not sent. It falls through to the App Store. The desktop slip joins ids with a comma, names the first event, and includes the event slug. A homepage link is dropped. Ids are not joined with `%2B` or a raw plus. Recheck the selection id when the number moves. A dead id adds nothing. A FanDuel selection uses addToBetslip with marketId and selectionId. Several FanDuel bets use marketId[n] and selectionId[n]. In a message those brackets are %5B and %5D, so the message does not cut them off. A shared FanDuel selection id still needs its market id, and the slip is confirmed before it is treated as placed. A DraftKings link is green and carries the DraftKings mark. A FanDuel link is blue and carries the FanDuel mark. The link color names the book. The alert color stays the rating. A Lean or a Pass does not get a link. Opening the link does not place the bet. Bet now means the price is posted and the kick is inside 15 minutes, or the live number still has room. The color is the rating: 🟢 5, 🟩 4, 🔵 3, 🟡 2, 🔴 1. One emoji says why. 💰 the posted number sits past the reads. 🏠 the matching-site games are the reason. ⚠️ the venue read is on the side and the last three or one game misses. 🛡️ a live lead is still inside the number. ⚡ the next score ends it. ➗ the reads do not agree. 📏 the sample is under 3. The rating is not a win probability, and 💰 is line value, not a priced edge. The sport mark leads the line: 🏈 football, 🏀 basketball, 🏒 hockey, ⚽ soccer, ⚾ baseball.
15. For a league with a thin public log, J1, J2, J3, FCS, and the same kind of league, an alert is incomplete until a local report is checked. Use the club site, the league preview, or a regional paper. Record the source, the date, and only what it says: starter or substitute, a quoted role, an injury, and a suspension. The note does not enter the four models. It can change the action. A player who is not starting does not stay a Bet.
16. The betting board is the alerts screen. It lists each Bet with the book link, the game progress, and the ticket status. A stake on a placed ticket is exposure. The potential gain is that ticket's own to-win. Settled gain adds a win and subtracts a loss. Those sums are not a parlay price.
17. A game script is its own view. Record personnel and scheme only from a named source and a date. A missing scheme stays missing. An absence, a debut, or a quoted workload opens a branch. The branch says what would confirm it and what would kill it. It does not enter the four models, the sim, or the book prices, and it does not change a Pass into a Bet.
18. Not every game gets the same research. Screen is the four reads, and a Pass stops there. A Bet on a public log is read against the matching-slice games before the link. A named game or a placed ticket gets sourced personnel and a game script. FCS, J1, J2, J3, and the same kind of league get a local report before that alert is final. A live game with no ticket stays at the clock and the number. A higher level does not enter the four models.
19. A scouting report lives in its own store. Record the source, the date, and only what the source said. The game script may read those reports. A scouted sim may drop a venue game only when a report names that team and that date, and the result stays labeled apart from the base sim. The four models and the base sim stay on the full log. Do not invent a snap share.
20. A card that names more than one sport answers every sport. Each sport gets its own block. A Bet carries the link. A sport with no Bet still gets one line and the reason: the number did not clear, the venue sample is under 3, or the board had no games. One sport's slip does not stand in for the others. A number already on a ticket stays that number. A later price on that same side is not a new bet, and the other side is not a hedge. A worse number does not get a link. For a favorite, laying more points is worse. For an under, the lower total is worse. For an over, the higher total is worse. When the number is the same, only the higher American price gets the link. The dated note is `docs/projections/MEMORY.md`.
