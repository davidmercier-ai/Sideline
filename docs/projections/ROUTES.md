# Routes, coverage, and personnel

This view does not enter the four models, the sim, or the book prices. It does not change a Pass into a Bet. A yard gap is not an edge.

The charted throw is the play file's pass length and pass location. That is not a route name. A slant, an out, or a go stays missing until a source names that route.

FTN charting names the box count, the pass rushers, and the blitzers. It does not name man or zone. Coverage stays missing.

A personnel look is favorable only when both samples are large enough and the receiver's own yards per target in the opponent's modal look are at least 1.5 higher than the other look. Eight targets on each side of the split. Twenty charted plays for the defense, and at least 40 percent in the modal look.

Run `node scripts/route-card.mjs`. The card is `docs/projections/2026-11-08-routes.md`.
